## 3. Full Description (पूरा विवरण - Copy & Paste into Uptodown)

```text
BatchCost Pro is the ultimate all-in-one business management app designed specifically for bakeries, home bakers, pastry chefs, cloud kitchens, and food entrepreneurs.

Stop guessing your food costs and profit margins! BatchCost Pro automatically calculates real recipe costs down to the gram, tracks wastage, manages raw ingredient inventory, and imports customer orders instantly.

⭐ KEY FEATURES:

🎂 Interactive Recipe & Batch Cost Sheet Builder
• Build custom batches from scratch or scale saved catalog recipes with exact yields.
• Real-time unit conversions across Mass (kg, g, mg), Volume (L, ml, cup, tbsp, tsp), and Count (piece, dozen, packet, box).
• Transparent step-by-step mathematical breakdown for every single ingredient.
• Comprehensive overhead allocation: Packaging, Labor hours, Oven electricity/gas, Delivery, and Wastage %.
• Accurate unit cost calculation: Total Batch Cost ÷ Number of Units.

💰 Margin vs Markup & GST Tax Calculator
• Distinguish true Profit Margin % (on revenue) from Markup % (on cost) with visual guidance.
• Integrated FSSAI / GST Tax Engine supporting 0%, 5%, 12%, and 18% tax slabs.
• Toggle between Tax-Inclusive and Tax-Exclusive pricing modes with itemized CGST + SGST breakdown.
• Instant 1-tap Cost Sheet export and WhatsApp sharing for kitchen teams and clients.

📲 WhatsApp & Diary Order Scanner
• Seamlessly scan handwritten order diaries or WhatsApp chat messages using camera and smart text recognition.
• Automatically extracts customer name, product ordered, quantity, and delivery date into your order book.

📦 Real-Time Pantry & Inventory Management
• Track raw ingredients (Flour, Butter, Sugar, Chocolate, Essence, Packaging boxes).
• Get instant low-stock alerts before ingredients run out.
• Live price history tracking to see supplier cost fluctuations.

☁️ Firebase Cloud Sync & Offline Support
• 100% offline-capable with local Room SQLite database.
• Automatic real-time backup to Firebase Firestore & Realtime Database.
• Never lose a customer order, recipe formula, or financial record.

🏷️ Nutrition Label & Price List Generator
• Generate high-resolution FSSAI-compliant food labels with ingredients, allergens, shelf life, and QR codes.
• Export professional wholesale and retail PDF / Image price lists for customers in seconds.

👥 Multi-Business & Staff Role Management
• Manage multiple outlets, cloud kitchens, or home bakery branches in one app.
• Assign roles for Master Chef, Billing Cashier, and Delivery Staff with access controls.

💼 Built For:
Home Bakers, Retail Bakeries, Cloud Kitchens, Pastry Shops, Caterers, Custom Cake Artists, and Small Food Businesses.
```
